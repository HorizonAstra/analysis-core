## How an analysis is built here

Two stages exist in this domain and only one of them is reachable today.

The first turns sequencing reads into the tables everything else works from. It
is heavy, it belongs on a cluster, and it is not offered here yet. A study that
holds only its reads, and no assembled tables, cannot be analysed here whatever
else is present. Say so plainly rather than working around it.

The second takes tables that already exist and asks what is associated with
what. Everything on offer sits here. These steps do not feed each other: each
reads the study's own tables and answers on its own, so there is no chain to
walk and no order to respect. What decides between them is the question, not a
sequence.

That means the layering to be careful about is not between steps but underneath
them: whether the values have been transformed, which level of resolution they
describe, and whether the samples are independent. Those are settled once, in
the paragraph before the first analysis, and every step afterwards inherits
them.
