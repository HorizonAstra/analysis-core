## What is being measured

Gene expression recorded at fixed positions across a tissue section. Position is
the point: the same expression table without coordinates would be a different
kind of data and would not support any of the questions asked here.

A position holds a small mixture of cells rather than one cell. Nothing in the
measurement says which cell type a signal came from, so a claim about cell types
needs a step that estimates them, and whether that step was run is a fact about
the study rather than something to assume.

The same sample can exist at several stages at once: what the instrument's own
pipeline produced, the matrix and labels prepared from it, and the results of
analyses already run over that. Which of them a sample has decides what can be
asked of it, and the answer varies from sample to sample within one study, so
find out what is actually there before planning around it.

Read those as things a sample has, not as a stage it is at. They accumulate
rather than replace each other, so a sample carrying the instrument's own run
has usually been prepared as well, and a sample lacking that run has not
outgrown it: the run was not brought here, which is a fact about what was
copied and not about the sample. Inferring a history from what is present is
how a sample gets described as unprocessed when the prepared files are sitting
right next to the thing being pointed at.

Samples are independent of one another unless a study says otherwise. There is
no repeated-measures structure here to correct for by default.
